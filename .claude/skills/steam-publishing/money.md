# Money

A working summary of Valve's terms as read on 2026-10-30. It is not tax advice; ask your accountant how to book this where you are.

- **Revenue share:** Valve keeps 30% of net revenue per game, dropping to 25% past USD 10M and 20% past USD 50M. Plan on 30%.
- **Payouts:** monthly, about 30 days after month end (February sales paid end of March), only when the month exceeds USD 100. Outside the US Valve pays by USD SWIFT wire, so a non-USD account receives it after the bank's conversion. [payments](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/finance/payments_salesreporting)
- **Reports:** Steamworks > Financial Information > Reports, monthly CSV with deductions. This is what an accountant needs each month.
- **US withholding:** on the US-sourced share only, at the rate the tax interview sets (0 to 30%). Without a treaty claim it is 30%. If your country has a US treaty, claim it with your company tax number as foreign TIN. Valve does not refund past withholding if the rate later drops, so get the interview right before the first sale. A 1042-S arrives by March 15. [tax FAQ](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/finance/taxfaq)
- **VAT:** Valve collects and remits VAT in the EU countries in its table; prices there include it and your share is on net revenue.
- **Fee:** USD 100 per game, recouped once the game passes USD 1,000 adjusted gross.
